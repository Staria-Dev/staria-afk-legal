Staria AFK Bot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Information Processed

The bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, AFK start times, and message events needed to provide its AFK features.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used to mark members as AFK, calculate their time away, announce their return, and clear their AFK status.

3. Storage and Retention

Information storage and retention depend on the bot's implementation and hosting setup. Data should only be retained for as long as needed to provide the service.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord or the bot's hosting provider as necessary to operate the service.

5. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You can stop interacting with the bot at any time. For questions or requests concerning information processed by the bot, contact its owner through the Discord server where it is used.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the Discord server where the bot is used.
