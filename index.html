Privacy Policy

The bot processes information needed to provide its AFK functionality.

This may include Discord user IDs, AFK start times, and server or message events needed to set or clear AFK status.

The bot uses this information to mark members as AFK, calculate how long they were away, and remove their AFK status when they return.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Data handling and retention depend on the bot's final implementation and hosting setup.

If you have a privacy question, contact the bot's owner through the Discord server where the bot is used.
